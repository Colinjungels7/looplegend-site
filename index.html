How It Works

From tee sheet to assignment, automatically

LoopLegend connects to your tee sheet and handles the rest. The caddie master stays in control — the system does the heavy lifting.

01

Tee Sheet Syncs Automatically

LoopLegend connects to your existing tee sheet software. Tee times, member names, walking or riding, guests, tournaments — it all flows in automatically. No double entry.

02

Caddies Set Their Own Availability

Caddies log in and set their exact available hours for each day — not just AM or PM. Set recurring weekly schedules or adjust specific dates. You always know exactly who's ready to work and when.

03

The Draft Engine Assigns Caddies

Every morning, LoopLegend drafts caddies to loops five days in advance — best caddies get the best loops, based on merit, ratings, availability, and member preferences. Caddies know their schedule for the week, not just the night before.

04

Gaps Fill Themselves

Caddie declines? Assignment expires? Someone cancels last minute? The system instantly finds a replacement. Within 6 hours of a tee time, it sends an emergency broadcast to every available caddie. First to accept gets it.

05

Everyone Gets Notified

Caddies get a text with their assignment and deadline to accept. Members get a confirmation when their caddie is locked in. The caddie master sees everything on one dashboard.

Features

Everything your caddie program needs

Full control for the caddie master. Transparency for caddies. Better service for members.

🏆

Merit-Based Draft Engine

Every morning, the system drafts caddies to loops based on ratings, acceptance rate, experience, and commitment. The caddies who earn it get the best loops.

📅

Self-Service Availability

Caddies manage their own schedules from their phone. Set exact available hours — not just AM or PM — so the system knows precisely when each caddie can work. No phone calls, no group texts, no guesswork.

🚨

Emergency Backfill

When someone cancels within 6 hours, every available caddie gets an instant broadcast. First to accept gets the loop. No scrambling required.

⭐

Member Caddie Requests

Members can request their favorite caddies directly through the system. They can also rate their experience after a round, which feeds into future assignments. The caddies members love keep coming back to them.

📊

Admin Dashboard

The caddie master sees the full tee sheet, every assignment, caddie status, and pending requests in one view. Add, remove, or reassign any caddie with a click.

📱

SMS Notifications

Caddies get assignment texts with a deadline to respond. Members get a confirmation when their caddie is locked in. Expiration warnings, emergency alerts — all automatic.

🔗

Tee Sheet Integration

Connects directly to your existing tee sheet software. Tee times, members, guests, walking or riding — it all syncs automatically. No double entry.

⏳

Tee Time Delay Alerts

Weather delay or course backup? The caddie master pushes tee times back and every affected caddie and member gets an instant text with their updated time.

📈

Performance Tracking

Track caddie ratings, loop counts, acceptance rates, and member feedback. Identify your top performers and see who's falling behind — all in real time.

Our Story

Built from the course, not a boardroom

LoopLegend started with a simple observation: caddie programs at private clubs run on manual effort, and it doesn't have to be that way.

Colin spent years caddying at a private club, watching the daily chaos of scheduling, communication, and last-minute changes that every caddie master faces. The best caddies didn't always get rewarded. Members didn't always get matched well. And when someone cancelled two hours out, it was pure chaos.

After years of thinking about the problem from inside the caddie program, he built LoopLegend — a system that brings structure, fairness, and transparency to the caddie experience without changing what makes it special.

LoopLegend was built on a simple belief: great technology should make tradition stronger, not replace it.
